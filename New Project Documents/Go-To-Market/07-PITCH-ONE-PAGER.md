## Understand your health — and what your care will actually cost — in one private place.

**The only personal health record with an AI guide that reasons over both your longitudinal health data *and* your real insurance costs — in a single loop.**

---

### The problem

Your health story is scattered across portals, PDFs, and lab printouts. Your medical costs are opaque — deductibles keep climbing, and you find out what something costs *after* you get the bill. And the new wave of AI health chatbots is impressive but generic: it doesn't know **your** labs, **your** plan, or **your** history.

More than a third of insured Americans now carry a single-coverage deductible of $2,000+, and families pay roughly $27,000 a year for coverage.¹ People are managing more of their own care and money than ever — with worse tools than they use to track a package.

### The solution

OwnMyHealth brings the two halves of your health life together in one member-owned, encrypted place:

- **📈 Your record, over time.** Drop in a lab PDF or connect supported labs — biomarkers are read automatically and tracked as a real trend line, flagged when they move out of range.
- **💸 Know before you go.** OwnMyHealth reads your insurance Summary of Benefits and projects your out-of-pocket cost against your actual deductible — so a procedure or visit isn't a surprise.
- **🤖 Ask your own data.** An AI guide that answers using *your* records and *your* plan — not a generic chatbot. Educational, never a diagnosis.
- **🔒 Yours, and private.** Per-user encryption, row-level isolation designed to fail closed, and full access logging. We don't sell your data.

### Why now

Deductibles and out-of-pocket costs are at record highs, new price-transparency and interoperability rules are putting your data and prices within reach, and consumers increasingly expect to *own* their health information. The one thing missing is a tool that connects "how am I trending?" to "what will it cost — and how do I pay less?" **No other consumer product welds those two together.**

### Trust, honestly stated

Privacy was engineered in from the start — per-user AES-256-GCM encryption, database row-level isolation designed to fail closed, and audit logging. We are building toward independent security attestation (e.g., SOC 2) and operate under a clear consumer privacy posture governed by the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state privacy laws (Washington My Health My Data Act, CCPA/CPRA). We do **not** claim certifications we don't yet hold, and we will publish our security and privacy practices plainly.
