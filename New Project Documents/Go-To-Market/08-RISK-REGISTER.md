# OwnMyHealth — Risk Register (D2C)

**Prepared 2026-06-20.** Scope: pure direct-to-consumer (B2C) consumer-health record + cost-navigation app. No employer / broker / benefits / PEPM channel anywhere in this analysis — the in-code FREE/PRO/TEAM "Team/family" tier is treated strictly as **consumer household sharing, not employer/seat licensing** (see R11).

**Likelihood / Impact scale:** Low / Med / High. **Impact = severity if it materializes** (High = company-ending or product-defining). Likelihood = probability over the next ~12 months on the current path.

**Sourcing convention (read this).** Every external market/competitor/benchmark/regulatory figure carries an inline bracketed tag keyed to the **Sources** footnotes at the end (e.g., `[RC25]`, `[FTC24]`). A tag means the number is a published third-party/source figure; vendor-benchmark roundups are directional (~), not audited. A claim marked **(estimate)** is a derived planning inference, not a sourced figure. A claim marked **(proposed anchor)** is a product/pricing decision we are choosing, not an external fact. Product/technical claims without a tag are verified against the codebase as read on 2026-06-20 or are stated in the MEASURED PRODUCT FACTS.

**Honest readiness baseline:** the product is, by its own UX review, a *"polished demo, not yet a trustworthy daily health record."* It holds **NO certifications** (only an Anthropic BAA is signed); has **no billing system**, **no MFA**, **no mobile app/PWA**; carries **two open High issues** (H-3 uncapped Document AI OCR spend; H-2 FHIR multi-instance requires `--max-instances=1` until Redis); **five PHI-path controllers are untested**; and known trust-breakers (placebo OCR review, biomarker time-series, dead goal-create, a11y islands) are remediated per changelog but **unverified end-to-end**. This register assumes that baseline — it does not assume the demo is shippable today.

## Go / No-Go Kill-Criteria Gates

Gates run **in order**. Do not advance to a later gate (or open the wallet on growth) until the earlier gate's criteria are met. This operationalizes the three-gate thesis (Compliance/Legal → Product-Hardening → Distribution/Growth) and the Tier-1 risks.

### Gate 0 — Compliance / Legal (before ANY paying consumer)
**No-Go until ALL true:**
- Breach-detection alerting live, routed to a **named** Security Officer / on-call; breach-response runbook exists (HBNR 60-day clock can start `[FTC24]`).
- BAAs signed (or proven PHI-free path) for **Google Cloud SQL/GCS + Document AI**; SendGrid PHI-free-or-swapped `[SG]`; Quest agreement in place before FHIR is enabled in prod.
- **L24 legacy plaintext PHI backfill run** and plaintext column dropped.
- **Zero** ad/analytics/tracker SDKs firing on any PHI path without explicit opt-in (the GoodRx/BetterHelp/Premom/Flo failure mode `[FTC-GR][FTC-BH]`).
- Standalone Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (WA `[KLGates]` + NV SB 370 `[Orrick]`) + general privacy policy + ToS + AI medical-advice disclaimer **published**; stale README "0 findings / fully encrypted" claims retracted.
- **TOTP MFA + recovery shipped** — treated as a Gate-0 must-have, not optional: this account *is* the user's medical record and insurance member IDs, account takeover = direct PHI breach, and R2 lists "no MFA" among the launch-blocking trust deficits. (Recovery codes + a tested no-support-human recovery path ship in lockstep so MFA can't create permanent lockouts.)

### Gate 1 — Product-Hardening (before public launch)
**No-Go until ALL true:**
- **Stripe self-serve billing live** (subscribe / cancel / failed-payment / receipts) — the Upgrade button is real.
- Onboarding funnel walked end-to-end on a fresh account with **every step doing real work**; the four known trust-breakers verified fixed **end-to-end** (not just per changelog).
- Controller/route tests cover the **five untested PHI/upload/AI/FHIR seams**.
- **H-3 Document AI OCR spend capped** in dollars; free-tier OCR pages bounded; plan-limit bypass (M12/M13) closed.
- **H-2 resolved** (Redis provisioned, `--max-instances=1` pin removed) **or** a documented launch-scale cap accepted.
- Installable **PWA** shipped (push + home-screen) — a real mobile habit surface exists.
- Activation instrumented ("first biomarker in session 1"; cost-surface engagement per R6/R17); closed beta passed without trust-breaker reports.

### Gate 2 — Distribution / Growth (before scaling spend)
**No-Go on paid acquisition until ALL true:**
- D7/D30 retention **at or above H&F benchmarks** `[Adapty26]`; ≥3 sessions in first 14 days for activated users.
- A working **referral loop** live with measurable K-factor > 0.
- Owned organic engines (content/SEO, ASO, founder/clinician authority) showing compounding traffic.
- **LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1** with CAC payback < 12 months on early cohorts — these are **chosen go/no-go thresholds** (the 3:1–4:1 healthy band is sourced `[Userpilot26]`; the specific gate is our decision).
- Paywall A/B infrastructure in place to run the harder-paywall pricing experiment on ARPU.

### Standing kill-criteria (any one = halt + reassess)
- A **breach or FTC/state-AG (incl. WA MHMDA) action** — existential; stop-the-line.
- Tracker/SDK discovered leaking health data → immediate remediation before any further launch.
- After a genuine, retention-first organic push, **LTV:CAC cannot clear 3:1** and no channel shows a path to it → the core D2C unit economics don't work; pivot or stop.
- A big-AI/cost incumbent **welds cost-navigation to a health record** and our fusion no longer tests as differentiated → revisit the wedge before further spend.
