## 2. Description of Service

OwnMyHealth is a privacy-focused health platform that lets you:

- **Track biomarkers**: enter, view, and analyze trends in lab values
  (cholesterol, glucose, vitamin D, DEXA results, etc.).
- **Manage insurance documents**: upload Summary of Benefits and
  Coverage (SBC) documents, compare plans, and search benefits.
- **Track health-related expenses**: record projected and actual
  out-of-pocket costs.
- **Set health goals**: create goals, log progress, view history.
- **Receive AI-generated educational guidance**: short,
  general-purpose explanations of biomarkers, insurance terms, and
  cost considerations, generated by Anthropic's Claude API.
- **Share with providers**: grant a healthcare provider scoped,
  consent-based access to your data, with granular permissions you
  can revoke at any time.
- **Export and delete your data**: download a portable copy of your
  data or permanently delete your account.

The Service is **not** a clinical record system, a diagnostic tool,
or a substitute for professional medical care. See §5.

We may change, add, or remove features at our discretion. Material
changes are announced in the [Changelog](./CHANGELOG.md) and, where
they affect data handling, in an updated Privacy Notice and a notice
to your registered email.

## 5. Health Information, AI, and "No Medical Advice"

### 5.1 You provide your own health data

OwnMyHealth is a tool **for keeping your own records**. You enter or
upload your data; we store it securely and help you organize it. We
do not generate clinical conclusions, do not adjudicate insurance
claims, and do not communicate with healthcare providers on your
behalf except where you explicitly grant a provider access through
the consent feature.

### 5.2 AI-generated content is educational

Parts of the Service use AI (Anthropic's Claude API, under a Business
Associate Agreement) to produce **educational explanations** —
descriptions of what a biomarker measures, what an insurance term
means, or how a cost estimate was calculated. These outputs are:

- **Not medical advice.** They do not diagnose, treat, cure, or
  prevent any condition.
- **Not a substitute for professional care.** Always discuss medical
  decisions with a licensed clinician.
- **Sometimes wrong.** AI can produce inaccurate or incomplete
  statements. You should verify any clinical claim against an
  authoritative source.
- **Educational only.** They are for general information and to help
  you have better-informed conversations with your provider.

We do not warrant the accuracy, completeness, or fitness for any
particular purpose of AI-generated content.

### 5.3 Emergencies

**The Service is not for medical emergencies.** If you think you are
experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency
number) or go to the nearest emergency room.

### 5.4 HIPAA and PHI

For details on how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your
Protected Health Information (PHI), see the
[Notice of Privacy Practices](./PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFT.md). Key points:

- We treat all health data you submit as PHI.
- We encrypt PHI at rest with AES-256-GCM, with per-user keys.
